Mode: multi-competitor comparison

## Differentiation Strategy

### Positioning: "Authentic Handcrafted Quality at Fair Price"

1. **Product Tier Strategy:**
   - Entry tier (2,980 JPY): Basic ceramic set, compete with Competitor C on value
   - Premium tier (4,980 JPY): Handcrafted + bamboo gift box, compete with Competitor B on quality
   - Average price lands where Competitor A sits

2. **Creative Advantage:**
   - Only competitor with a main image video (Competitors A & B have none)
   - Show the ceramic-making process (craftsmanship story)
   - Japanese tea ceremony demonstration

3. **Keyword Strategy:**
   - Avoid head-term wars (dominated by A & C)
   - Own: "handcrafted matcha set", "ceramic tea ceremony kit", "gift matcha set"
   - These terms have lower volume but match our differentiation

4. **Promo Calendar:**
   - Launch: 20% coupon (30 days) + Vine enrollment
   - Month 2: Reduce to 10% coupon
   - Month 3: Switch to multi-buy discount (set + matcha powder bundle)
