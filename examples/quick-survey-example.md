# Example: 30-Minute Quick Survey

## Scenario

You are a seller planning to launch a **smart pet water fountain** on **Amazon US**.
You want a quick competitive landscape before committing to full research.

## Request

```
@amazon-competitor-analysis
Quick survey mode: ASIN B0C6LQPM8M on Amazon US
My product: smart pet water fountain, UV sterilization + app control + quiet pump, $39-59
Mode: quick survey (modules 1, 3, 4 only)
```

## Expected Output (Summary)

### Module 1: Basic Info Card

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Brand | PetKit |
| Core ASIN | B0C6LQPM8M |
| Listing Date | 2023.06 |
| Current Price | $49.99 |
| Rating / Reviews | 4.4★ (2,847 reviews) |
| Monthly Sales | ~3,200 units |
| BSR | Pet Supplies #127 / Cat Fountains #3 |
| Est. Conversion | 8.2% |
| Variants | 3 (1.6L / 2.5L / 3.0L) |

### Module 3: Ad Strategy Summary

| Ad Type | Active Campaigns | Top Keywords |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|
| SP | 8 campaigns | "cat water fountain", "pet fountain", "stainless steel cat fountain" |
| SB | 2 campaigns | Brand terms + "smart pet fountain" |
| SBV | 1 campaign | Product demo video |

**Keyword Distribution:**
- Head terms: 35% (competitive, high volume)
- Mid-tail: 45% (balanced ROI)
- Long-tail: 20% (niche, high conversion)

**Key Takeaway:** Competitor is heavily invested in SP with broad keyword coverage. Mid-tail strategy is the sweet spot.

### Module 4: Promotion Summary (Keepa 90-day)

| Promo Type | Discount | Frequency | Duration |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|
| Long-term | 5% coupon | Always on | Year-round |
| Short Deal | 15% off | ~Monthly | 7 days |
| Price Cut | None | — | — |

**Price Range (90 days):** $44.99 (lowest) — $49.99 (regular)

**Key Takeaway:** Competitor uses small but consistent coupon rather than deep discounts. Pricing is stable — brand-led strategy, not price-war.

## Actionable Insights (from 30 min)

1. **Price positioning**: Enter at $39.99-44.99 to undercut while maintaining margin
2. **Keyword gap**: "UV sterilization pet fountain" has low competition — target as differentiator
3. **Promo playbook**: Start with 10% launch coupon, transition to 5% evergreen
4. **Ad strategy**: Focus on mid-tail "stainless steel pet fountain" keywords (lower CPC, good intent)
